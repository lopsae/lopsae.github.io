FrameCutter
-----------

Frame Cutter is an iOS app that can extract a frame from any video in the Photos Library. All metadata available in the original video is exported along the frame:
+ Location of the video is copied.
+ Camera information is derived from the information in the video.
+ The time of the frame is adjusted based on the time of the video and the time of the frame itself.

Available in the App Store.
