FrameCutter Privacy Policy
--------------------------

Frame Cutter does not collect or store any personal information. All processing of videos and images happens entirely on your device.

Frame Cutter may request access to add to or read your Photo Library, this access is used only for saving images into your Photo Library, and optionally adding the images to an specific folder. The Photo Library access is not used in any other way.

Frame Cutter does not use any third party services for the purpouse of analytics, advertisement, or tracking.


### Updates to the Policy

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date at the bottom of this Privacy Notice.
